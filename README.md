# 📊 Sales Analysis Dashboard | Data Analytics Project

## 📌 Project Overview

The **Sales Analysis Dashboard** is a business intelligence and data analytics project designed to monitor, analyze, and visualize sales performance across different regions, product categories, sales channels, customer types, and time periods.

The project transforms sales data into an interactive visual report that enables business stakeholders to evaluate revenue performance, track order activity, understand product demand, compare regional sales, and identify opportunities for business improvement.

The dashboard presents key performance indicators (KPIs), trend analysis, regional comparisons, and product and sales channel visualizations in one centralized interface.

**Project Type:** Data Analytics & Business Intelligence  
**Domain:** Sales and Retail Analytics  
**Dashboard Tool:** Microsoft Excel, based on the dashboard's visible interface  
**Project Status:** Dashboard developed; further analysis and validation can be added as needed.

## 💼 Business Problem

Businesses generate large volumes of sales data, but raw records alone do not always provide a clear understanding of business performance.

Without an effective reporting system, managers may find it difficult to determine which regions generate the most revenue, which product categories perform best, how sales change over time, or which sales channels contribute most to overall revenue.

This project addresses that challenge by presenting important sales metrics and visual comparisons in an interactive dashboard.

The dashboard helps users move from reviewing individual transactions to understanding broader business trends and performance indicators.

### Key Business Questions

1. What is the total revenue generated by the business?
2. How many orders were recorded?
3. What quantity of products was sold?
4. What is the average order value?
5. Which geographical regions generate the highest revenue?
6. Which product categories contribute most to sales?
7. How does revenue change over time?
8. How does revenue vary across sales channels?
9. How can sales performance be explored across different dates?
10. What opportunities exist to improve sales and business performance?

*Note: Values are transcribed from the dashboard screenshot. Confirm the currency, aggregation methods, and calculations against the source dataset before treating them as final business results.*

### KPI Definitions

**1. Total Revenue**

Measures the total revenue generated from sales transactions during the selected reporting period.

**2. Total Orders**

Measures the number of sales orders recorded. The calculation should count unique order IDs if an order can contain multiple product rows.

**3. Quantity Sold**

Measures the total units sold across the selected products and reporting period.

**4. Average Order Value (AOV)**

Measures the average revenue generated per order.

Formula:

Average Order Value = Total Revenue ÷ Total Orders

This metric helps businesses understand the average monetary value of an order.

## 💡 Business Recommendations

Based on the dashboard's design and the initial observations, the following are potential recommendations for further investigation.

### 1. Investigate Regional Performance

Examine the factors contributing to the stronger performance of North and Central, and determine whether their successful practices can be applied in other regions.

### 2. Review Product Category Performance

Investigate high-revenue and low-revenue categories using sales volume, profit margin, inventory levels, and customer demand before making product investment decisions.

### 3. Monitor Revenue Trends

Track revenue over consistent reporting periods and investigate unusual increases or decreases to support forecasting and sales planning.

### 4. Evaluate Sales Channels

Compare channels using revenue, order volume, average order value, acquisition costs, and profitability to determine where additional resources may be most effective.

### 5. Strengthen Customer Retention

Compare new and returning customers using repeat purchase rates, spending patterns, and order frequency to identify opportunities for stronger customer relationships.

### 6. Improve KPI Monitoring

Review the dashboard regularly and use consistent KPI definitions so business stakeholders can compare performance accurately over time.

*These are analytical recommendations, not confirmed outcomes. Their effectiveness should be evaluated using the underlying data and additional business context.*
